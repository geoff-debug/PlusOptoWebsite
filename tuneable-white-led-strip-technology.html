Tuneable White LED Strip Technology

How tuneable white (CCT) LED strips blend warm and cool white light to set any colour temperature, and how to choose, wire and control them.

Overview

What is a tuneable white LED strip?

A tuneable white strip, also called CCT or CT adjustable strip, carries two independent channels of white LEDs: one warm white and one cool white. By changing the brightness of each channel, a controller blends the two to give any colour temperature between them, from a warm, relaxing glow to crisp, cool daylight, and dims the overall output at the same time.

Our tuneable white range includes dual-LED 2835 and 3014 strips at 120, 224, 228 and 252 LEDs/m, 2-in-1 3838 and 5050 strips with a warm and cool chip in every package, and dot-free SOB and COB versions. Strips are available in 12V and 24V, with CRI 80, 90 or 95, and work with our range of CCT controllers, from simple RF remotes to DALI DT8 and Casambi systems.

The technology

How colour tuning works

The strip has three connections: a common positive (V+) and two negative channels, one for the warm white LEDs and one for the cool white LEDs. A two-channel CCT controller sits between the power supply and the strip and switches each channel using PWM (pulse width modulation).

With only the warm channel on, the strip gives its warmest colour temperature; with only the cool channel on, its coolest. Mixing the two in different proportions moves the colour smoothly between them, while scaling both channels together dims the light without changing its colour.

The power rating is quoted with both channels fully on, so size the power supply for the combined maximum load.

Control & installation

Getting the best from tuneable white

  • Use a two-channel CCT controller matched to the strip voltage and the current of each channel.
  • Size the power supply for both channels on, using the maximum rating such as 24W/m, plus a safety margin.
  • Choose 24V for longer runs to reduce voltage drop, which can shift colour as well as brightness along the strip.
  • Diffuse dual-LED strip with an opal profile cover if it will be seen directly; choose 2-in-1, SOB or COB for shallow or open mounting.
  • Mount on aluminium profile at higher power ratings to keep the LEDs cool and colour stable.
  • Keep channels consistent across multiple runs so every strip tunes to the same colour.

Tuneable white questions
