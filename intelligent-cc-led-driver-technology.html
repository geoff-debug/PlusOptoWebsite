Intelligent CC LED Driver Technology

How programmable, multi-protocol constant current drivers let one driver suit many LED modules and control systems, and how to choose the right one.

Overview

What is an intelligent CC LED driver?

An intelligent constant current (CC) driver has its output current and settings programmed rather than fixed. Most models are set by NFC from a phone app or programmer, in steps as small as 1mA, so one driver can be matched to many different LED modules. Multi-protocol models also accept several dimming inputs, so the same driver suits DALI, 0-10V or simple push switch projects.

Our range from Ltech and Lifud covers 8W to 75W and 100mA to 1800mA. It includes the multi-protocol Ltech SE-65-500-1800-W2X2, NFC programmable DALI-2 SE-W1D and tuneable white SE-W2D drivers, compact DIP switch SE-W1DS drivers, the Lifud LF-GSD060YC with DALI, 0-10V and push dimming, and the Lifud LF-ACD075D for DALI D4i luminaires.

The technology

How intelligent CC drivers work

Inside the driver, a microcontroller stores its settings: the output current, the dimming interface, the DALI address and options such as fade time or dimming curve. An NFC programmer or phone app writes these settings, and the same profile can be copied to many drivers in seconds.

On multi-protocol drivers, the control input can be DALI-2, 0-10V (or 1-10V, PWM and resistor) or a mains push switch, chosen to suit the project.

The driver then regulates the programmed constant current into the LED module and dims it with high-frequency PWM, down to 0.01% on Ltech SE models.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and setting up an intelligent CC driver

  • Pick the control system: DALI-2 DT6 for dimming, DT8 for tuneable white, D4i for smart luminaires, or a multi-protocol driver for flexibility.
  • Match the current range: choose a driver whose range includes the LED module's rated current.
  • Check the voltage window: the module's forward voltage must sit within the driver's output range at that current.
  • Program before power-up: set the current by NFC or DIP switch before connecting the LED load.
  • Save a profile: store the settings in the app and copy them to every driver of the same luminaire type.
  • Address DALI drivers: assign addresses and groups from the DALI controller, or by NFC where supported.

FAQ

Intelligent CC driver questions

It is a constant current LED driver whose output current and settings are programmed rather than fixed, and which accepts more than one dimming input. The Ltech SE-65-500-1800-W2X2, for example, accepts DALI-2 DT6 or DT8, 0-10V (also 1-10V, PWM and resistor) and push dimming, with the current set by NFC in 1mA steps.

An NFC programmer or a phone app with NFC is held against the driver to read and write its settings. Depending on the model, you can set the output current in steps down to 1mA, the dimming interface, the DALI address and other parameters, then copy the same settings quickly to other drivers.

DT6 is the DALI device type for single channel LED dimming. DT8 adds colour control, such as colour temperature for tuneable white, as used by the SE-W2D and SE-65-500-1800-W2X2. D4i is a DALI-2 standard for intelligent luminaires that report energy and diagnostic data and can power sensors, as on the Lifud LF-ACD075D with its 24V auxiliary output.

One programmable driver can be set to suit many different LED modules, so fewer driver types need to be stocked and the light output of each luminaire can be matched exactly. Setting the current in 1mA steps also allows fine adjustment of brightness between luminaires.

The Ltech SE series dims from 100% down to 0.01% with T-PWM at 3600Hz, flicker free to IEEE 1789. The Lifud LF-GSD060YC dims down to 0.1%.
