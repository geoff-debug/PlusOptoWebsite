IGBTs, MOSFETs & Power Modules

Considered an ideal semiconductor switch, we offer a range of IGBT (Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistors), MOSFETs and PIM's (Power Integrated Modules) for a wide range of power switching applications. These devices offer high efficiency, fast switching for high power applications and are available in a range of packages.

IGBT Discrete Devices

Industry standard TO package discrete IGBT devices combining technology from a MOS gate and a bipolar transistor, optimising the component for high voltage and high current applications. Devices also offer Trench-Gate Field-Stop technology providing greater control of dynamic characteristics with a lower Collector-Emitter Saturation Voltage for fewer switching losses. AEC-Q100 certified.

IGBT Industrial Modules

Products include PIM (Power Integrated Module), CIB (Convertor Invertor Break), Six Pack or Full Bridge, Half Bridge, Single Switch and Break Chopper configurations. AEC-Q100 certified.

Power Integrated Modules (PIM)

A range of PIM's in industry standard housings with high speed Planar-FS IGBT and Fast Recovery Diode chips. Half-bridge modules with increased blocking voltage to 1200V, low switching losses, positive temperature coefficient and low reverse recovery charge. AEC-Q100 certified.

IGBT Automotive Modules
