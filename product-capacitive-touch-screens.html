Capacitive Touch Screens

Projected Capacitive Touch Screens (PCAP) with a G+G (Glass+Glass) structure and scratch-resistant surface hardness of ≥6H (500gf), available from 3.5-inch to 12.1-inch with 5 or 10-point touch control.

3.5" 320x240 PCP Touch Screen

The DS-CS035DSA-01A is a 3.5-inch capacitive touch module (PCAP, Projected Capacitive Touch Screen) with a resolution of 320×240 pixels. It supports 5-point touch control, ensuring smooth and accurate touch operation. Featuring a G+G (Glass+Glass) structure and surface hardness of ≥6H (500gf), it is scratch-resistant and durable.
