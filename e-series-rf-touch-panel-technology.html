E Series RF Touch Panel Technology

How LTECH E series wall touch panels control LED lighting by direct output, DMX512 or 0-10V, with 2.4GHz RF for remotes and receivers.

Overview

What are E series RF touch panels?

E series panels are LTECH glass touch panels that fit a standard 86mm wall box. Unlike battery panels, they are wired to power and give a fixed wall control with touch sliders, colour wheels, zone keys and scene keys.

The range offers three ways to reach the lights. The E1 to E4S panels drive 12–24V LED strip directly from their terminals. The EX panels send a DMX512 signal to DMX decoders and drivers. The E1S-AD and ECT2 send 0-10V to dimmable drivers. Every panel also has a 2.4GHz RF radio, so it works with LTECH hand-held remotes, RF receivers and the L-BUS app through a WiFi gateway.

  • Glass touch panel for a standard 86mm wall box
  • Direct CV output, DMX512 or 0-10V
  • 2.4GHz RF to remotes, receivers and gateways
  • Dimming, CCT, RGB and RGBW control
  • Scene keys that save and recall lighting states

The technology

How E series panels work

A capacitive touch surface reads the keys and sliders. The panel then sends the result to the lights in up to three ways at once.

Wired output: direct PWM to the LED strip on the E1 to E4S, a DMX512 signal on the EX panels, or a 0-10V signal on the E1S-AD and ECT2.

2.4GHz RF: the same commands go wirelessly to matched LTECH receiver drivers, with sync so dynamic modes stay in step. Remotes and a WiFi gateway can control the panel in the same way.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and setting up an E series panel

  • Choose the output: direct CV for small runs, DMX512 or 0-10V to match larger drivers.
  • Match the light type: dimming, CT, RGB or RGBW to suit the strip.
  • Check the load: the direct-drive panels give up to 4A per channel (3A on RGBW), 288W maximum.
  • Fit the panel: wire the power and output, then fit it to a standard 86mm wall box.
  • Match a remote: long press the panel's on/off key until its indicator flickers, then long press on/off on the remote.
  • Add the app: match the panel to an LTECH WiFi gateway in the L-BUS app's Zone Set screen.

E series panel questions

An E series panel is an LTECH glass touch panel that fits a standard 86mm wall box. It is wired to power and controls LED lighting from the wall, and it also has a 2.4GHz RF radio, so it can be used alongside LTECH hand-held remotes, RF receiver drivers and the L-BUS app through a WiFi gateway.

The E1 to E4S panels run from 12-24VDC and drive constant voltage LED strip directly from their output terminals. The EX panels run from mains and send a DMX512 signal to DMX decoders and drivers. The E1S-AD and ECT2 run from mains and send a 0-10V signal to 0-10V drivers. All of them also send 2.4GHz RF to compatible receivers.

The directly wired E1 to E4S panels deliver up to 4A per channel, or 3A per channel on the 4-channel RGBW models, with a maximum of 288W at 24V. For larger loads, use an EX panel with DMX decoders, a 0-10V panel with 0-10V drivers, or add RF receiver drivers.
