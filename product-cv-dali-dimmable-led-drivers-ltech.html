Ltech CV DALI Dimmable LED Drivers

DALI (Digital Addressable Lighting Interface) is an International Standard for the control of DALI enabled lighting systems. Our Ltech range covers constant voltage DALI dimmable LED drivers from 36W to 150W, including DALI-2 DT8 colour temperature tuneable and 4-channel DALI/DMX RGBW models, all with additional mains push dimming.
