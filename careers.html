Careers at Plus Opto

Join the UK's Optoelectronic Specialists

Since 1994, we have specialised in the application and supply of optoelectronic components to the UK electronics manufacturing industry — specifically LEDs, displays and control solutions.

As a market leader in the supply of optoelectronic components, our company has a long-standing reputation and experience in providing products and application solutions into a diverse range of market sectors, ranging from global "blue chip" companies to medium sized and small entrepreneurial companies that adopt the latest technologies.

We have a number of positions available for skilled and motivated individuals who wish to progress their career development. We offer a modern working environment with a friendly, professional approach and a competitive salary.

Current Vacancies

Technical Support Engineer

We seek a Technical Support Engineer to liaise with our sales team and customers to provide support and assistance for our range of components & assemblies. This will involve identifying component suitability for applications, determining alternatives and solutions to existing devices, generating technical information and gaining customer approvals. The role is internally based at our offices in Leigh, Lancashire where full training will be provided.

Leigh, Lancashire

Product Manager – Displays

We seek a motivated individual for the position of Product Manager aligned to our established and growing optoelectronics display business. The role incorporates the management and coordination of our existing business, development of opportunities and maintaining and communicating product information from our extensive portfolio of LCD based products to our sales team and customers.

The position requires experience within the electrical/electronics industry, a good level of technical knowledge, product understanding and the ability to absorb new information. Full training will be given on the specific product portfolio and processes. Good communication and computer skills are also essential to this role, and there is the opportunity for personal development within the business.

Leigh, Lancashire
