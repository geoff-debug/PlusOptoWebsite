DALI Touch Panel Technology

How DALI touch panels switch, dim and colour-tune LED lighting over the two-wire DALI bus, by address, group or broadcast.

Overview

What are DALI touch panels?

DALI touch panels are glass wall controls that connect to the DALI bus, the two-wire control line that links DALI LED drivers. They do not switch the LED power themselves. Instead they send digital commands to the drivers, which then switch, dim and colour-tune the lights.

Each panel is powered from the bus, so only the two DALI wires run to the wall box. The panel can control drivers by individual address, by group or by broadcast, and its scene keys recall lighting scenes stored in the drivers. The panels follow the DALI standard, IEC 62386, so they work with DALI drivers of the matching device type.

  • Glass touch panel for a standard 86mm wall box
  • Powered from the DALI bus, with no separate supply
  • Unicast, group and broadcast control
  • Dimming (DT6) and colour control (DT8)
  • Scene keys that recall stored DALI scenes

DALI panels at a glance

  • Standard: IEC 62386
  • Power: DALI bus, under 6mA
  • Device types: DT6, DT8
  • Control: Dim, CT, RGB, RGBW
  • Zones: Up to 4 per panel
  • Addresses: 64 short, 16 groups
  • Size: 86 × 86mm
  • Ranges: DP, EDT, EDA

The technology

How DALI panels work

A DALI bus power supply puts around 16V on the two bus wires. The panel and the drivers all connect to the same pair, which is polarity-free and can run alongside the mains cabling.

When a key or slider is touched, the panel sends a DALI command on the bus. Every driver hears it, but only the drivers with the matching short address or group respond, so one pair of wires can control many separate zones.

Scenes are stored in the drivers themselves. A short press on a scene key recalls the scene, and a long press saves the current light levels to it.

Benefits

Advantages of DALI panels

Why a DALI wall panel suits commercial and larger lighting schemes.

Two wires only

Just the polarity-free DALI pair runs to the wall box, with no mains switching.

Bus powered

The panel draws its power from the DALI bus, so there are no batteries or adapters.

Flexible zoning

Control single addresses, groups or the whole bus, and rezone without rewiring.

Open standard

IEC 62386 commands work with DALI drivers of the matching device type.

Scenes

Recall up to 16 stored scenes, and save new ones with a long press.

Colour control

DT8 panels tune white light and colour as well as brightness.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and setting up a DALI panel

  • Match the device type: DT6 for dimming, DT8 for tunable white or colour, to suit the drivers.
  • Power the bus: fit a DALI bus power supply, for example 16V at 250mA, and wire DA1 and DA2 to the panel and drivers.
  • Address the drivers: give each driver a short address (0–63) or add it to a group (0–15).
  • Set the panel mode: choose unicast, group or broadcast and the start value. The four zone keys take that value and the next three.
  • Set the scenes: choose the first scene number. The scene keys take that scene and the ones that follow.
  • Fit the panel: fix the base to an 86mm wall box and clip the glass panel on.

FAQ

DALI panel questions

A DALI touch panel is a wall control that connects to the two-wire DALI bus instead of switching mains or LED power. It sends DALI commands to the drivers on the bus to switch, dim and colour-tune the lights, and it recalls scenes stored in the drivers. It fits a standard 86mm wall box.

No separate supply is needed at the panel. It is powered from the DALI bus itself, drawing less than 6mA. The bus does need a DALI bus power supply, such as a 16V, 250mA unit, which also powers the other control devices on the bus.

In unicast mode each zone key controls one driver short address (0 to 63). In group mode each zone key controls one DALI group (0 to 15), so many drivers respond together. In broadcast mode the panel controls every driver on the bus. The panel's four zone keys take four consecutive addresses or groups from the start value you set.

They are DALI device types defined in IEC 62386. DT6 is for single-channel LED dimming. DT8 is for colour control, using Tc for tunable white, x-y coordinates for RGB, or RGBWAF for multi-channel colour. The panel's control type must match the device type of the DALI drivers.

On the EDT and EDA panels, two rotary switches on the back select the mode and the start address: X sets unicast, group or broadcast and Y sets the value. On the DP panels, unlock the digital display with a long press of the M key, then step through the address, group and scene settings with the arrow keys.
