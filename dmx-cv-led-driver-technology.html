DMX CV LED Driver Technology

How DMX512 constant voltage drivers power and dim single colour, tuneable white and RGBW LED strip in one unit, and how to address, wire and size them.

Overview

What is a DMX CV LED driver?

A DMX constant voltage (CV) driver combines a mains LED power supply and a DMX decoder in one unit. It provides a 12V or 24V output for LED strip and dims each output channel by PWM to the levels it receives on its DMX channels. One driver replaces a separate power supply and decoder, saving space and wiring in the luminaire or ceiling void.

Our range from Ltech and Lifud covers 75W to 150W in slim linear cases. Single channel LM-G1M2 and NFC programmable LM-G1M2F drivers dim single colour strip, the 2-channel LM-G2M2 controls tuneable white, and the 4-channel LM-150-24-G4K3 and Lifud LF-GBD150 drive RGBW strip and also accept DALI-2. All dim down to 0.1%, flicker free, and accept a mains push switch.

The technology

How DMX CV dimming works

A DMX controller sends a universe of 512 channels, each with a level from 0 to 255, along a daisy-chained data cable with a 120Ω terminator at the end.

Each driver is set to a start address and uses one channel per output: one for single colour, two for tuneable white and four for RGBW. An RGBW driver at address 10 reads channels 10 to 13.

The driver holds its output at 12V or 24V and switches each channel by high-frequency PWM, so the strip dims smoothly and mixes colours without flicker.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and installing a DMX CV driver

  • Match the voltage: use a 12V driver for 12V strip and a 24V driver for 24V strip.
  • Size the power: add up the strip load (W/m × length) and allow at least 20% headroom; on multi-channel drivers, check each channel's current too.
  • Plan the addresses: allow 1, 2 or 4 channels per driver so their address ranges don't overlap, unless they should act together.
  • Daisy-chain the cable: run screened twisted-pair data cable from driver to driver, with no spurs or star joints.
  • Terminate the line: fit a 120Ω terminator at the last driver on each DMX line.
  • Limit voltage drop: on long strip runs, feed from both ends or from several points.

FAQ

DMX CV driver questions

A DMX constant voltage (CV) driver is a mains power supply and DMX decoder in one unit. It provides a 12V or 24V output for LED strip and dims each output channel by PWM to the levels received on its DMX channels, so no separate power supply or decoder is needed.

A driver uses one DMX channel per output channel, starting at its address: one for single colour strip, two for tuneable white (dim and colour temperature) and four for RGBW. For example, an RGBW driver set to address 10 uses channels 10, 11, 12 and 13.

The address can be set remotely by RDM from a compatible DMX controller. On the Ltech LM-G1M2F drivers, a phone app can also set the address, dimming curve and brightness range by NFC.

Some can. The Ltech LM-150-24-G4K3 accepts DMX512/RDM or DALI-2 DT8, and the Lifud LF-GBD150-6250-24 accepts DALI-2 or DMX512/RDM, so the same driver can be used in either system. Both also accept a mains push switch.

Match the output voltage to the strip and add up the strip load in watts (watts per metre multiplied by length), allowing at least 20% headroom. On multi-channel drivers, also check that the load on each channel stays within its current rating.
