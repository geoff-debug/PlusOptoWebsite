About Ligitek

Who is Ligitek Electronics?

Ligitek Electronics Co., Ltd. was established in Taiwan in 1989 and is headquartered in New Taipei City. Ligitek designs, develops and manufactures a broad range of optoelectronic components for a wide variety of applications.

The Ligitek range covers through-hole LEDs, PCB type, PLCC and leaded SMD LEDs, smart LEDs with integrated control, infrared emitters, photo transistors and photo diodes, automotive interior and exterior LEDs, LED arrays, LED backlights and single to four digit LED displays in through-hole and SMD formats.

Ligitek holds IATF 16949 automotive quality certification together with ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.
