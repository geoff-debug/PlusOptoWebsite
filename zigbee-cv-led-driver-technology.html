Zigbee CV LED Driver Technology

How Zigbee constant voltage drivers bring smart home and app control to LED strip, and how to choose between mains drivers and low-voltage receivers.

Overview

What is a Zigbee CV LED driver?

Zigbee is an open global wireless standard for low-power smart home and building devices. A Zigbee constant voltage (CV) driver powers 12V or 24V LED strip and has a Zigbee radio built in, so the strip can be switched, dimmed and colour controlled from a smartphone app, a Zigbee remote or wall panel, or a voice assistant, with no control wiring.

Our range has two types. The Lifud LF-GAZ150 is a 150W mains driver with a 24V output and Zigbee control in one unit, with a 2-channel tuneable white version. The MiBoxer FUT-Z series are Zigbee 3.0 receivers that fit between a 12–24V power supply and the strip, in dimming, CCT, RGB, RGBW and RGB+CCT models.

  • App control with the Tuya Smart app through a Zigbee gateway
  • Voice control via the gateway with popular voice assistants
  • Self-healing Zigbee 3.0 mesh: each driver extends the network
  • Single colour, CCT, RGB, RGBW and RGB+CCT control
  • Flicker-free dimming to IEEE 1789 on the LF-GAZ150

The technology

How Zigbee control works

A Zigbee gateway (or hub) creates the network and connects it to your WiFi router, so the Tuya Smart app and voice assistants can send commands from anywhere.

Each mains-powered driver acts as a router in the Zigbee 3.0 mesh, passing messages on to its neighbours. Adding drivers extends the range, and messages find another path if one device is out of reach.

The driver holds a steady 12V or 24V output and dims each channel by PWM. Zigbee remotes and wall panels can also be paired to control drivers directly.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and installing a Zigbee CV driver

  • Choose the type: a mains driver for all-in-one installations, or a receiver to use with a separate 12V or 24V power supply.
  • Match the channels: 1 for single colour, 2 for CCT, 3 for RGB, 4 for RGBW and 5 for RGB+CCT.
  • Size the load: add up the strip load (W/m × length) with at least 20% headroom, within the driver or receiver rating.
  • Add a gateway: install a Zigbee gateway for app and voice control, and pair each driver to it.
  • Plan the mesh: keep drivers within range of each other or the gateway, and avoid enclosed metal housings.
  • Add switches: pair Zigbee remotes or wall panels for local control.

Zigbee CV driver questions

Zigbee 3.0 is the current version of the Zigbee standard. It brings the earlier application profiles together into one, so certified devices from different manufacturers can join the same network. It runs in the 2.4GHz band on the low-power IEEE 802.15.4 radio standard.

Mains-powered Zigbee devices, including LED drivers, act as routers that pass messages on to other devices. Each driver added to the network therefore extends its coverage, and messages can take another route if one device is out of range.

A mains Zigbee driver such as the Lifud LF-GAZ150 combines the power supply and Zigbee control in one unit. A low-voltage Zigbee receiver such as the MiBoxer FUT-Z series fits between a separate 12V or 24V power supply and the strip, and offers dimming, CCT, RGB, RGBW and RGB+CCT models.
