MiBoxer Remote Control Technology

How MiBoxer 2.4GHz hand-held remotes control LED strip and fittings through FUT series receivers, and how to choose the right remote.

Overview

What are MiBoxer remote controls?

MiBoxer (formerly Mi-Light) remotes are battery-powered 2.4GHz wireless controllers for LED lighting. They send commands to MiBoxer receiver drivers, which dim and colour-mix the LED strip or fittings connected to them, so lights can be switched, dimmed and colour controlled from anywhere in the room with no control wiring.

Our range covers single colour, CCT, RGB+CCT and SPI pixel control, from single zone up to 100 zones. It includes the C series, FUT088, FUT089 and FUT092 hand-held remotes, the FUT006 and FUT007 4-zone dimmers, the S series puck-style remotes, the FUT100 with an LCD display, and the C6 for SPI pixel strip.

  • 2.4GHz RF with around 30m control distance
  • Zone control from single zone up to 100 zones
  • Dimming, CCT, RGB, RGBW and RGB+CCT control
  • One remote can control many receivers in sync
  • Works with FUT receivers, WiFi controllers and touch panels

The technology

How MiBoxer remotes work

Each remote has its own ID. When you press a button, it sends a short 2.4GHz message with its ID, the zone and the command. A receiver only responds to remotes and zones it has been linked to.

Each zone button controls the receivers linked to it, so one remote can set different areas to different levels and colours, or switch them all together.

Receivers relay the signal to other receivers within 30m, which extends the range and keeps dynamic effects in sync across several controllers.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and setting up a MiBoxer remote

  • Match the light type: a dimming, CCT or RGB+CCT remote to suit the strip and receiver.
  • Count the zones: one zone per area you want to control separately.
  • Link each receiver: power-cycle the receiver, then press the zone's on button three times within a few seconds.
  • Group receivers: link several receivers to the same zone to control them together.
  • Mind the range: around 30m in open space; walls and metal enclosures reduce it.
  • Unlink if needed: power-cycle the receiver and press the zone's on button five times.

FAQ

MiBoxer remote questions

MiBoxer (Mi-Light) 2.4GHz remotes work with MiBoxer FUT series receiver drivers, LS2 and CL series receivers, compatible WiFi controllers such as the WL5, and MiBoxer touch panels. They use MiBoxer's own 2.4GHz protocol, so they will not control other brands of RF receiver.

Switch the receiver's power off and on again, then within a few seconds press the on button of the zone you want, usually three times. The lights blink to confirm the link. To unlink, repeat the power cycle and press the zone on button five times. The exact steps are in each receiver's instructions.

A zone is a group of receivers that the remote controls together. A 4-zone remote can switch, dim and set the colour of four areas separately, or all at once. MiBoxer remotes range from single zone up to 100 zones on the FUT100, which has an LCD display to show the selected zone.

MiBoxer remotes have a control distance of around 30 metres. Receivers also pass the signal on to other receivers within 30 metres, which extends the effective range and keeps dynamic effects in sync across several controllers.

Match the remote to the type of light and the number of zones: a dimming remote for single colour strip, a CCT remote for tuneable white, or an RGB+CCT remote for colour strip, which can also control dimming and CCT receivers. Then pick a style, such as the hand-held C or FUT series or the puck-style S series.
