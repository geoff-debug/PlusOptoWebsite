TFT Cable Customisation

We offer custom FPC, FFC, wire-harness and signal-conversion solutions for different product structures and motherboard interfaces.

Overview

We offer custom FPC, FFC, wire-harness and signal-conversion solutions for different product structures and motherboard interfaces.

Connection Methods

LCD screen cables are generally connected in two ways:

  • Solution 1: the FPC is bonded directly to the LCD, and the customer uses a matching connector to connect with the display
  • Solution 2: the LCD module retains a connector, while an FPC cable, FFC cable or wire harness carries the signal to the motherboard

The direct-FPC route keeps the product compact and is often suitable for handheld or space-limited products. The connector-and-cable route is better when the motherboard and display are separated, when the transmission distance is longer or when vibration resistance is important.

FPC

FPC shape, length, pin definition and pin pitch can be customized to match different mechanical layouts and interface requirements. Electromagnetic shielding film can also be used when improved EMI immunity is required.

The FPC circuit can be optimized through filtering, anti-interference design, layout adjustment and impedance matching to improve signal stability for different application environments.

Cable

Cable length, material and connector type can be adapted to match different interface requirements. When signal quality needs additional protection, shielding, grounding or a magnetic ring can be added.
