OLED Touchscreen Displays

OLED graphic displays with integrated capacitive touch panels, combining high contrast, wide viewing angles and rapid response with on-glass touch sensing. Ideal for wearables, smart home appliances and smart healthcare devices where a compact, single-chip touch and display solution is required.

0.91" 128x32 OLED In-Cell Touch Panel

WEO012832M-CTP is a 0.91" COG Graphic OLED display with In-Cell Capacitive Touch Panel, with resolution of 128x32 pixels. The built-in SSD7317 TDDI IC (Touch with Display Driver IC) supports 4-Wire SPI and I2C interface. This SSD7317 IC is a Touch and Display Driver Integration IC (TDDI IC), which integrates touch and display microelectronics into a single chip for use on PMOLED panels, targeting applications including wearables, smart home appliances, and smart healthcare devices.

1.18" 128x128 OLED Round Display Module with Touch Screen

WEO128128D-CTP, a Ø1.18" round graphic OLED display with a capacitive touch screen integrated into the module, with a resolution of 128x128 dots. The module is equipped with the SSD1327 IC, supporting a 4-line SPI and I2C interface, with a typical logic supply voltage of 3V and a 1/128 driving duty. Additionally, the WEO128128D-CTP model includes the FT3268 touch panel IC integrated into the module, supporting the I2C interface and featuring a single detect point for the capacitive touch screen.
