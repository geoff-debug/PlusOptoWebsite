About Lifud

Who is Lifud?

Lifud Technology Co., Ltd is an LED driver manufacturer headquartered in Shenzhen, China. The Lifud brand was established in 2007, and the company has industrial parks in Zhongshan, Guangdong Province and in Sichuan Province, with R&D centres in Shenzhen, Chengdu and Zhongshan and UL and DALI certified laboratories.

Lifud's range covers compact, linear, in-track, industrial and outdoor LED drivers, constant voltage drivers, and lighting controls such as sensors, CCT controllers and DMX512 decoders.
