B Series RF Touch Panel Technology

How battery-powered B series touch panels control LED lighting wirelessly through MiBoxer receivers, and how to choose the right panel.

Overview

What are B series RF touch panels?

B series panels are MiBoxer (Mi-Light) wall controls with a capacitive glass touch surface. They look and feel like a wired wall panel, but they run on two AAA batteries and send 2.4GHz RF commands to MiBoxer receivers. The receivers then dim and colour-mix the LED strip or fittings connected to them.

Because there is no cable between the panel and the lights, a panel can be stuck to any flat surface in minutes. That makes them ideal for retrofit work and for adding a second control point. Our range covers single colour, CCT, RGBW, RGB+CCT and SPI pixel control, with single, 4-zone and 8-zone models.

  • Capacitive glass touch panel, 86 × 86mm
  • 2.4GHz RF with around 30m control distance
  • Battery powered: no wall box or wiring
  • Single zone, 4-zone and 8-zone models
  • Dimming, CCT, RGBW, RGB+CCT and SPI control

The technology

How B series panels work

Under the glass, a capacitive touch IC senses your finger on the buttons and sliders. The panel then sends a short 2.4GHz message with its own ID, the zone and the command.

Each receiver responds only to the panels and zones it has been linked to. One panel can therefore set different areas to different levels and colours, or switch them all together with ALL ON and ALL OFF.

The panel draws only microamps on standby, so two AAA batteries last a long time.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and setting up a B series panel

  • Match the light type: a dimming, CCT, RGBW, RGB+CCT or SPI panel to suit the strip and receiver.
  • Count the zones: one zone per area you want to control separately.
  • Fit the panel: stick the holder to the wall, fit 2 × AAA batteries and attach the panel.
  • Link each receiver: switch it off for 10 seconds and back on, then press the zone ON button three times within 3 seconds.
  • Mind the range: around 30m in open space; walls, metal and strong magnetic fields reduce it.
  • Unlink if needed: power-cycle the receiver and press the same zone ON button five times.

B series panel questions

A B series panel is a battery-powered wall panel with a capacitive touch surface. It looks like a wired wall control, but it sends 2.4GHz RF commands to MiBoxer (Mi-Light) receivers, which dim and colour-mix the LED strip or fittings connected to them. No cable is needed between the panel and the lights.

Stick the holder to the wall with its adhesive back, fit two AAA batteries, and attach the panel to the holder. No wall box, mains supply or control cable is needed, so the panel can go on any flat surface, including glass or tiles.

Switch the receiver off for 10 seconds and back on. Within 3 seconds, press the zone ON button (or ON on the B0) three times. The light blinks three times slowly to confirm the link. To unlink, repeat the power cycle and press the same button five times, and the light blinks ten times quickly.

Match the panel to the receiver and LED strip: the B1 or B5 for single colour dimming, the B2 for tuneable white, the B3 for RGB or RGBW, the B4, B0 or B8 for RGB+CCT, and the B6 for SPI pixel strip with an SPIR3 controller. Then choose the number of zones: the B0 is single zone, most panels have 4 zones, and the B8 has 8.
