Dimmable LED Drivers

Dimmable LED drivers supporting DALI, Casambi, 0-10V, triac, DMX, Bluetooth and Zigbee protocols.

To support our extensive range of LED products we offer a wide range of dimmable LED drivers in Constant Voltage and Constant Current output mode. A choice of dimming protocols is available including DALI, DALI 2, Casambi, 0-10V, Triac, DMX, Bluetooth and Zigbee. All drivers feature short circuit, temperature and over-current protection and conform to international safety standards.
