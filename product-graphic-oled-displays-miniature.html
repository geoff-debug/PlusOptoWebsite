Miniature OLED Displays

Miniature OLED graphic displays in ultra-thin, lightweight construction for applications prioritising compact size. Ideal for handheld instruments, wearable electronics, personal care and medical devices. Features include rapid response times, wide viewing angles, high brightness and contrast, extended temperature operation, minimal power draw and sunlight readability.

Ultra-thin COG graphic PMOLED with 96×32 dot resolution. Integrates SSD1315 IC and supports 6800/8080 8-bit parallel, I²C, and 4-wire SPI interfaces. Logic supply 3V, 1/32 duty cycle, typical display current 3.6mA @ 12Vcc. Available in ZIF FPC and Hotbar versions.
