About Signify

Who is Signify?

Signify N.V., formerly Philips Lighting, is a global lighting company headquartered in Eindhoven, the Netherlands. It was renamed Signify in 2018 and continues to sell lighting products under the Philips brand.

Signify's OEM components business supplies the building blocks that luminaire makers design in: Fortimo LED light engines and modules, Xitanium LED drivers, and connected lighting systems such as MasterConnect with EasyAir sensors and switches.
