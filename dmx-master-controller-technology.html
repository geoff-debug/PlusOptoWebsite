DMX Master Controller Technology

How DMX master controllers generate the DMX512 signal that drives LED decoders and drivers, from USB interfaces to pixel controllers and wall panels.

Overview

What are DMX master controllers?

A DMX master controller is the device that creates the DMX512 signal. DMX decoders and DMX LED drivers only follow instructions, so every DMX lighting system needs one master at the start of the line.

The master sends a level from 0 to 255 for each of up to 512 channels, refreshed many times a second. Each decoder reads the channels from its own start address and drives its LEDs to match. Masters range from USB interfaces programmed from a PC, to pixel controllers that play video shows, to wall panels with touch keys.

  • Generates the DMX512 signal for decoders and drivers
  • 512 channels per universe, up to 1024 on dual-universe models
  • Programmed from PC software or set from the panel
  • Stand-alone playback from internal memory or SD card
  • Scene triggers from keys, timers, contacts and remotes

DMX masters at a glance

  • Protocol: DMX512
  • Channels: 512 per universe
  • Types: USB, pixel, wall panel
  • Software: LED Player
  • Playback: PC or stand-alone
  • Pixel outputs: 16 (LT-600)
  • Wireless: 2.4GHz RF, WiFi
  • Brands: LTECH, MiBoxer

The technology

How a DMX master works

DMX512 is a one-way serial signal sent over a twisted data pair. The master sends a packet holding the level of every channel, then repeats it continuously, so a decoder that misses one packet catches the next.

The decoders are daisy-chained on one line. Each is set to a start address and takes as many channels as it needs: 3 for RGB, 4 for RGBW, 5 for RGB+CCT. A 120Ω terminator at the end of the line stops signal reflections.

Shows are built in PC software or chosen on the panel, then stored in the master and replayed with no computer attached.

Selection & set-up

Choosing and setting up a DMX master

  • Count the channels: add up 3, 4 or 5 channels per decoder. Over 512 needs a second universe.
  • Choose the control: PC software for custom shows, a wall panel for day-to-day room control.
  • Wire the line: daisy-chain the decoders with screened twisted-pair cable and fit a 120Ω terminator at the end.
  • Address the decoders: set each start address to match the channels the master sends.
  • Programme the show: build scenes in LED Player and save them to the controller's memory.
  • Add triggers: assign scenes to keys, times, contacts or a remote for stand-alone use.

FAQ

DMX master questions

A DMX master controller is the device that generates the DMX512 signal. It sends a level for each of up to 512 channels many times a second, and the DMX decoders and drivers on the line each read the channels at their own address. Without a master, DMX decoders have nothing to follow.

A universe is one DMX512 line carrying 512 channels. An RGB decoder uses 3 channels, an RGBW decoder 4 and an RGB+CCT decoder 5, so one universe can control up to 170 RGB zones. Larger projects use controllers with two or more universes, such as the LTSA-1024 with 1024 channels.

Only for programming. The USB interfaces are programmed from LED Player or similar software, then the scenes are saved to their internal memory and played in stand-alone mode with no computer attached. The LT-600 plays shows from its SD card, and the X5 and MS-WF-512 wall panels work on their own from the wall.

Run a single daisy chain from the master to each decoder in turn using screened twisted-pair data cable, not a star or branches. Fit a 120 ohm terminator across the data pair at the last decoder. A DMX line normally supports up to 32 devices before a DMX amplifier or splitter is needed.

Set each decoder's start address so its channels match what the master sends. For example, the X5 RGB+CCT panel uses 5 channels per zone, so the decoders are set to 001 for zone 1, 006 for zone 2, 011 for zone 3 and 016 for zone 4. With PC software, you choose the addresses when you add the fixtures to the project.
