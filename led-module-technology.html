LED Module Technology

How LED chain modules light signs, channel letters and lightboxes evenly, and how to choose the right module, spacing and power supply for your design.

Overview

What is an LED chain module?

An LED chain module is a small, self-contained light source: one or more LEDs mounted on a rigid circuit board with their own current control, sealed in a plastic housing and protected by potting or encapsulation. Modules are joined by flexible wires into chains, so they can be laid out in rows across the back of a sign, inside a channel letter or behind a lightbox face, and powered together from a single low-voltage supply.

Our range runs at 12VDC and covers single-LED mini modules for small letters, multi-LED 2835 modules up to 720lm for large lightboxes, 5050 modules in white and RGB, and an FPC module for minimal spotting. Modules are rated IP65 or IP67, with wide 120° to 175° beam angles, and fix with 3M adhesive tape and a screw hole.

  • Even, efficient backlighting for signs, letters and lightboxes
  • Wide-angle optics spread light across the sign face
  • IP65 and IP67 sealed construction for outdoor signage
  • Parallel-wired chains that can be cut and joined to suit
  • Mono-colour white and RGB colour-changing options

The technology

How modules light a sign

Modules are fixed in rows to the back panel of the sign, facing the translucent front face. Each module's wide-angle optic spreads light outwards, and the light from neighbouring modules overlaps before it reaches the face, so the sign glows evenly rather than showing individual bright spots.

The two things that control evenness are depth (the distance from the modules to the face) and spacing (the distance between modules). A deeper box gives the light more room to spread, so modules can be placed further apart. Shallow boxes need closer spacing or wider beam angles.

Every module in a chain is wired in parallel across the 12V supply, so each one receives the same voltage and the chain can be cut or extended between any two modules.

Benefits

Advantages of LED modules

Why sign makers choose LED modules over fluorescent tubes and LED strip for backlighting.

Even illumination

Wide-angle optics up to 175° spread light across the face, so fewer modules give a bright, uniform sign without hotspots.

Flexible layout

Wired chains bend around letter shapes and can be spaced to suit any outline, from narrow strokes to large lightboxes.

Built for outdoors

Sealed IP65 and IP67 housings protect the LEDs from moisture and dust in exterior signs.

Low energy, low heat

Efficient 2835 and 5050 LEDs use far less energy than fluorescent tubes, with less heat inside the sign.

Quick to install

3M adhesive tape and a screw hole on every module make fixing fast and secure, with no soldering needed.

White or colour

Mono-colour white modules for classic signs, and RGB modules for colour-changing and dynamic effects.

Design guide

Planning a module layout

  • Measure the depth from the back panel to the face. Deeper boxes allow wider spacing and fewer modules.
  • Choose the module for the depth and size: mini modules for narrow letters, high-output modules for large or deep lightboxes.
  • Lay modules in even rows, keeping spacing between rows similar to spacing along the chain, and keep modules back from the edges to avoid bright borders.
  • Calculate the power: number of modules x watts per module, plus around 20% margin, to size the 12V supply.
  • Feed long chains at more than one point to avoid voltage drop and dimmer modules at the far end.
  • Seal cut wire ends and use outdoor-rated drivers and connectors for exterior signs.

Power supply example

A lightbox using 100 x PWM4417 modules at 0.72W each:

  • Module load: 100 x 0.72W = 72W
  • With 20% margin: approx. 86W
  • Suggested supply: 100W, 12VDC

LED module questions

An LED chain module is a small, sealed LED light source with one or more LEDs on a rigid PCB, potted or encapsulated for protection. Modules are joined by flexible wires into chains so they can be spaced out across the back of a sign, channel letter or lightbox and powered from a single 12V supply.

Spacing depends on the depth of the lightbox or letter and the beam angle of the module. As a general guide, deeper boxes allow wider spacing because the light has more distance to spread, while shallow boxes need modules closer together or wide-angle modules to avoid hotspots.
