OLED Touchscreen Technology

Graphic OLED displays from 0.91" to 5.5" with capacitive touch built in. Find out how the touch panel is added to an OLED, the difference between add-on and in-cell touch, and how to choose a structure and interface.

Overview

An OLED touchscreen display combines a self-emitting graphic OLED with a capacitive touch panel (CTP) in a single module. The OLED gives deep blacks, high contrast and wide viewing angles with no backlight, and the touch panel senses a finger through the cover glass, so the display can also be the control panel. That suits compact products such as smart home controls, appliances, wearables and medical devices.

As the authorised distributor for Winstar Display Corporation we offer OLED touch modules from the 0.91" 128x32 with in-cell touch up to the 5.5" 256x64, including a 1.18" round display. Modules come as bare COG or COF glass, or mounted on a frame and PCB, in white, yellow, sky blue or green.

OLED Touch at a Glance

  • Technology: Monochrome PMOLED + capacitive touch
  • Sizes: 0.91" to 5.5"
  • Resolution: 128x32 to 256x64, 128x128 round
  • Display ICs: SSD1309, SSD1322, SSD1327, SSD7317
  • Display interfaces: 6800, 8080, SPI, I2C
  • Touch: 1 finger; in-cell keys and slide on 0.91"
  • Colours: White, yellow, sky blue, green
  • Operating temp: Typically -20℃ to +70℃

How OLED Touch Works

A capacitive touch panel carries a grid of transparent electrodes. Your finger changes the electric field between them, and the touch controller works out where it is. Because the panel senses through glass, it needs no pressure and has no moving parts.

On most of the range the touch panel is a separate layer on top of the OLED, with its own touch controller, joined to the display by air bonding or optical bonding. The 0.91" uses in-cell touch instead: the sensing is built into the OLED panel and a single TDDI chip drives the display and reads the touch.

Advantages of OLED Touchscreens

Why designers choose an OLED with touch for compact control panels

High Contrast

Unlit pixels are truly black, so icons and text stand out clearly, even through a touch panel.

Wide Viewing Angles

Self-emitting pixels look the same from almost any angle, ideal for wall and appliance controls.

Buttons Replaced by Glass

Touch keys on the display replace mechanical buttons, for a sealed, easy-clean front.

Thin & Compact

No backlight layer, and the in-cell 0.91" is just 1.41mm thick, suiting wearables and slim devices.

Single-Chip Option

The SSD7317 TDDI on the 0.91" drives display and touch from one chip, saving parts and space.

Ready to Mount

Frame and PCB versions add mounting holes and a pin header, making them simple to fit.

Choosing a Structure

COG (chip on glass) and COF (chip on film) modules are the thinnest, with the driver on the glass or on the flexible cable, ready to fit behind your own front panel.

Frame and PCB versions mount the display on a board with a metal frame, screw holes and a pin header, for easy fitting and hand assembly.

Choosing an Interface

The display and the touch panel connect separately on most models. The display interface depends on the driver IC, from I2C for the fewest wires to a parallel bus for the fastest updates. The touch controller usually reports over I2C.

Frequently Asked Questions

It is a graphic OLED display with a capacitive touch panel (CTP) on the same module. The OLED pixels give out their own light, so the display has deep blacks, high contrast and wide viewing angles, while the touch panel detects a finger through the cover glass so the display can also work as the control panel.

In-cell touch builds the touch sensing into the OLED panel itself instead of adding a separate touch glass. The 0.91" WEO012832M-CTP uses the SSD7317, a TDDI (touch and display driver integration) IC that drives the display and reads touch from a single chip. It supports four touch keys and a left or right slide, in a module just 1.41mm thick.

With air bonding the touch panel is fixed around its edges, leaving a thin air gap above the display. With optical bonding (OCA) the gap is filled with optically clear adhesive, which cuts reflections, improves readability and makes the front stronger. The 1.54" WEO012864A-CTP is available with either option.

On most models the touch panel has its own controller on a separate connection, using I2C and detecting one finger. For example, the 1.54" and 3.55" use the FT6336U and the 1.18" round display uses the FT3268. The display itself connects separately over SPI, I2C or a 6800/8080 parallel bus, depending on the driver IC.

These are monochrome OLEDs, offered in white, yellow, sky blue or green depending on the model. The 256x64 models with the SSD1322 driver and the 1.18" round display with the SSD1327 support greyscale, for smooth icons and graphics.
