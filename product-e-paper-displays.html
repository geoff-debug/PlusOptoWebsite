E-Paper Displays

Winstar's Electronic Paper Display (EPD) incorporates "bistable" technology, allowing images to persist even when the power source is removed, consuming power only during E-paper screen updates. This E-paper display technology offers a wide range of advantages including ultra-low power consumption and excellent readability in direct sunlight.

1.54" EPD 200x200 with Front Light

WAA0154A2AAA4NXXX000 is an Electronic Paper Display (EPD) with a front light panel. The 1.54" active area contains 200 x 200 pixels and supports 1-bit black-and-white display with full display capabilities. It offers excellent readability under various lighting conditions and is ideal for applications requiring low power consumption and wide viewing angles. The display integrates a gate buffer, source buffer, interface, timing controller logic, oscillator, DC-DC converter, SRAM, LUT, VCOM, and borders for each panel.

2.13" EPD 122x250 4-colour module

WAA0213A2CNA6NXXX000 is a colour EPD module built on an active matrix TFT substrate, designed for applications requiring low power consumption and high contrast. The panel can display black, white, yellow, and red images, depending on the associated lookup table. Leveraging color epaper display technology, this technology provides exceptional reflectivity and ultra-wide viewing angles, ensuring clear and highly readable content, even in bright environments.

The WAA0270A2AAA7NXXX000 is a 2.7" EPD equipped with an integrated front light panel, delivering a high-contrast black-and-white reflective display with exceptional readability and ultra-low power consumption.
