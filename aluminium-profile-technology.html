Aluminium Profile Technology

How aluminium profiles house, cool and protect LED strip, and how to choose the right profile and diffuser for a smooth, even line of light.

Overview

What is an aluminium profile?

An aluminium profile is an extruded aluminium channel that LED strip is stuck into. The aluminium body acts as a heat sink, drawing heat away from the LEDs, while a clip-in or slide-in diffuser covers the strip to soften the light and protect it from dust and knocks. End caps close off each end and mounting clips or brackets hold the profile in place.

Crucial to the performance of linear LED fixtures, our aluminium extrusions are compatible with a wide range of LED strip in a choice of colours and white CCTs. They also improve heat dissipation to manage LED strip temperature and extend LED lifetime.

We offer several types of aluminium channel to suit different applications, including commercial, cabinet display, office and home lighting. Our range covers surface mount, recessed, corner, round, suspended / pendant, plaster-in wall mount, mini and lensed profiles for low-voltage LED strip, plus surface-mount profiles with an integrated hand sweep sensor. Each comes with a choice of diffuser, from clear to opal and milky finishes.

  • Heat dissipation for longer LED life and stable light output
  • Mechanical protection for the LED strip
  • Diffusers that soften the light and hide individual LEDs
  • A straight, neat, professional finish
  • Surface, recessed, corner, round, suspended and plaster-in mounting

How a profile fits together

How it fits together: the LED strip adheres to the flat base of the aluminium channel, which draws heat away from the LEDs. A snap-in diffuser covers the strip to soften the light and hide individual LED dots, and end caps close off each end for a neat finish.

  1. Aluminium channel: the extruded body. The LED strip sticks to its flat base, which acts as a heat sink.
  2. LED strip: cut to length at the marked cut points and connected at the solder pads.
  3. Diffuser: snaps or slides in over the strip to soften the light and hide individual LED dots.
  4. End caps: close off each end for a neat finish, with a cable entry where needed.

Diffusers

Choosing a diffuser

The diffuser is the cover that clips or slides into the profile over the LED strip. It sets both how much light comes out and how smooth that light looks.

A clear diffuser lets almost all of the light through but shows each LED as a bright dot. Frosted, opal and milky diffusers scatter the light so the dots blend into a continuous line, at the cost of some output.

As a rule, choose a clearer diffuser where the profile is out of sight and maximum brightness matters, and a more diffusing one where the profile is in direct view.

Diffuser comparison

Light output against diffusion

The more a diffuser scatters the light, the smoother the line looks, and the less light gets through.

Profile types

Choosing a profile

Match the profile shape to how and where it will be mounted.

Surface mount

Fixes directly onto any flat surface with adhesive or clips. The simplest option for most installations.

Recessed

Sits in a routed slot or linear recess so the diffuser finishes flush with the surface for a seamless look.

Corner

Right-angled profile for internal corners, aiming the light at an angle for directional lighting.

Round

Round lens for wide-angle illumination, suited to under-cabinet and display applications.

Suspended / pendant

Hung over a work or utility surface on suspension wires for linear pendant lighting.

Plaster-in wall mount

Set into drywall and plastered over so only the diffuser shows, giving a seamless line of light flush with the wall or ceiling.

Mini

Compact surface mount or recessed profiles for narrow LED strip, where space is tight and a slim line of light is needed.

Lensed

A lens cover in place of a diffuser focuses the light from the LEDs into a narrower beam angle, for accent, wall-washing and long-throw lighting.

Sensor profiles

Surface mount profiles such as the SAP-YD1205HS with an integrated hand sweep sensor for touchless control in furniture and cabinets.

Design guide

Getting a smooth line of light

  • Check the strip width fits inside the profile channel.
  • Choose the diffuser for the viewing position: clearer where the profile is hidden, opal or milky where it is in direct view.
  • Use a deeper profile to increase the distance between the LEDs and the diffuser for a more even line.
  • Pair with a high-density or COB strip for a continuous, dot-free appearance.
  • Allow for the diffuser loss when choosing strip output: a 60% diffuser needs a brighter strip than a 90% one for the same result.
  • Cut profile and diffuser to the same length and finish with end caps.

Output example

An LED strip rated at 1000lm per metre:

  • Transparent (approx. 90%): approx. 900lm/m
  • Opal frosted (approx. 60%): approx. 600lm/m
  • Glossy milky (approx. 50%): approx. 500lm/m
