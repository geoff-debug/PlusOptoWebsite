About Macroblock

Who is Macroblock?

Macroblock, Inc. was founded in June 1999 in Hsinchu, Taiwan. It is a mixed-signal driver IC design house focused on power management and opto-electronic applications, providing LED driver ICs and power management ICs.

Macroblock's range covers LED display driver ICs for fine pitch, mini-LED and micro-LED displays, and LED lighting driver ICs for full-array local dimming backlights, RGB lighting, and automotive lighting and displays, together with DC/DC, AC/DC and linear regulator solutions. Macroblock is certified to ISO 9001:2015.
