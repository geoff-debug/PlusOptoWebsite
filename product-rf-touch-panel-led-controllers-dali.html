DALI Touch Panel LED Controllers

Our range of DALI touch controllers offer a cost effective and easy to install solution for LED lighting control. The panels support dimming, CT, RGB and RGBW control and are easily mounted into a standard single gang wall plate. Use in conjunction with DALI LED drivers

DP1S / DP2S / DP3S
DP1 to DP3 DALI Touch Panels
The DP1S to DP3S Touch Panel LED controllers are designed to drive LED strips and clusters via DALI bus. Supports 1 to 3 Channel control using DALI DT6 / DT8 standard protocol to IEC62386 with easy to set address.
