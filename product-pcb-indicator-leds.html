PCB Indicator LEDs

To facilitate ease of assembly we offer a wide range of PCB Indicators based on discrete LEDs packaged in UL94-V0 rated housings, in surface mount and through-hole options with single, 2-tier, 3-tier and 4-way configurations.

LA304B Surface Mount PCB Indicators

Single-way indicators built on 3.0mm and 5.0mm round LED lamps designed for surface mount assembly, in UL94-V0 rated housings with clear, tinted or diffused lens options.

View Details →

LA16B/LA93B 3.0mm and 5.0mm PCB Indicators

Single-way through-hole PCB indicators available in 3.0mm and 5.0mm sizes, with water clear, tinted or diffused lens options and a wide viewing angle.

View Details →

LA45B/LA44B 2-Tier PCB Indicators

Two-tier through-hole PCB indicators, in UL94-V0 rated housings, with interlocking versions for compact multi-status indication.

View Details →

LA73B 3-Tier PCB Indicators

Three-tier through-hole PCB indicators, in UL94-V0 rated housings, with interlocking versions for stacked multi-status indication.

View Details →

LA79B 4-Way PCB Indicators

Four-way through-hole PCB indicators, in UL94-V0 rated housings, with interlocking versions for extended multi-status indication.
