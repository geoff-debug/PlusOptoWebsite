LED Modules

LED chain modules designed for signage applications including channel letter backlighting, face and halo illumination.

Overview

Using the latest generation of high efficient LED emitters we offer a range of LED chain modules designed for signage applications including channel letter backlighting, face and halo illumination. Options include: Low, mid and high power ratings in IP20 or Waterproof IP67. A choice of colours is available including full colour RGB and choice of white colour temperature. Our design team supports custom designs and a choice of connection, drive & control solutions is available.
