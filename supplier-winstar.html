About Winstar

Who is Winstar Display?

Winstar Display Co., Ltd. is a display manufacturer headquartered in Taichung, Taiwan. Winstar designs and produces a wide range of display modules for industrial, medical, automotive, instrumentation, consumer and smart-home applications, from small character LCDs and OLEDs through to large high-brightness TFTs and complete smart display systems.

The Winstar portfolio covers TFT LCD modules, OLED displays, character and graphic LCDs, e-paper, touch panels and smart displays with built-in controllers, along with development boards that help engineering teams prototype display-based products quickly.
